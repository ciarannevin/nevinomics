Financial

Highlights

Rates are annualised agreed rates on new business (AAR) for lending and deposits, and levels for ECB policy rates; changes in percentage points (pp). Credit is outstanding amounts (stocks) in EUR millions. Ireland data as reported by the Central Bank of Ireland; euro area aggregates shown where relevant. Source: ECB Data Portal (FM key rates, MIR interest rates, BSI balance sheet items).

ECB policy rates

Deposit facility rate and main refinancing operations rate, euro area, daily, since 2015, percent per annum. Source: ECB Data Portal (FM, series FM/D.U2.EUR.4F.KR.DFR.LEV, FM/D.U2.EUR.4F.KR.MRR_FR.LEV).

New lending rates: households

Interest rates on new loans to households, annualised agreed rate, percent per annum, monthly, since 2015: mortgages (lending for house purchase) and consumer credit for Ireland, mortgages for the euro area. Source: ECB Data Portal (MIR, series MIR.M.IE.B.A2C.A.R.A.2250.EUR.N, MIR.M.IE.B.A2B.A.R.A.2250.EUR.N, MIR.M.U2.B.A2C.A.R.A.2250.EUR.N).

New lending rates: non-financial corporations

Cost of borrowing (annualised agreed rate) on new loans to non-financial corporations, percent per annum, monthly, since 2015, Ireland vs euro area. Source: ECB Data Portal (MIR, series MIR.M.IE.B.A2I.AM.R.A.2240.EUR.N, MIR.M.U2.B.A2I.AM.R.A.2240.EUR.N).

Household deposit rates

Interest rates on new household deposits, annualised agreed rate, percent per annum, monthly, since 2015: overnight and agreed-maturity deposits for Ireland, overnight for the euro area. Source: ECB Data Portal (MIR, series MIR.M.IE.B.L21.A.R.A.2250.EUR.N, MIR.M.IE.B.L22.A.R.A.2250.EUR.N, MIR.M.U2.B.L21.A.R.A.2250.EUR.N).

Loans outstanding

Outstanding amounts of loans to Irish households for house purchase and to non-financial corporations, EUR millions, end of month, since 2015. Source: ECB Data Portal (BSI, series BSI.M.IE.N.A.A22.A.1.U2.2250.Z01.E, BSI.M.IE.N.A.A20.A.1.U2.2240.Z01.E).

Annual growth in lending

Annual growth rate of outstanding loans to households for house purchase and to non-financial corporations, percent, since 2015 (derived from end-of-month stocks). Source: ECB Data Portal (BSI), as above; growth derived by nevinomics.

Net mortgage lending

Net lending (financial transactions) for house purchase to Irish households, EUR millions, trailing 3 months, since 2015. Source: ECB Data Portal (BSI, series BSI.M.IE.N.A.A22.A.4.U2.2250.Z01.E).
