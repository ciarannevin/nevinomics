About Nevinomics

What this site is, where the data comes from, and what's next.

What is Nevinomics?

Nevinomics is a dashboard of economic indicators for Ireland, with European and global context planned. It is a personal learning project, built to make official statistics easy to read and share.

Every chart carries the nevinomics watermark, so shared screenshots are always traceable back to the site.

Data sources

  • GDP growth — Central Statistics Office, Quarterly National Accounts (table NAQ04): seasonally adjusted, constant prices. Updated via the free CSO API.

Planned sources: CSO (labour market, CPI, government finance), Eurostat (European comparisons), World Bank & IMF (global context), Central Bank of Ireland (rates).

Roadmap

  1. Live: GDP growth (Output page)
  2. Output: GNI*, industrial production
  3. Labour Market: unemployment rate, participation
  4. Inflation: CPI/HICP, energy prices
  5. Fiscal: exchequer returns, debt-to-GDP
  6. Financial: ECB rates, mortgage rates
  7. Global: Ireland vs Eurozone/OECD
