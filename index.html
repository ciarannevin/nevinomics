About the data

Sources

All data comes from official sources: the Central Statistics Office (national accounts, labour market, prices) and the Central Bank of Ireland Quarterly Bulletin (forecasts). Neither organisation is responsible for this site.

Cadence

The site updates as the official statistics are published — monthly for prices and unemployment, quarterly for the national accounts and labour force survey. Figures are revised when the sources revise them.

Methodology

Growth rates are computed from seasonally adjusted, constant-price series where available. Forecasts are clearly distinguished from outturns. Read more on the About page.
