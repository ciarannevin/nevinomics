Highlights

Revenue, expenditure and balance: seasonally adjusted quarterly general government transactions, EUR millions. Debt: gross general government debt (EDP face value) at end of quarter. Debt to GNI*: end-year debt as a share of Modified Gross National Income (our derivation from CSO data). Forecasts: Central Bank of Ireland Quarterly Bulletin, percent of GNI*. Sources: Central Statistics Office (Tables GFQ01, GFQ02, NA001); Central Bank of Ireland Quarterly Bulletin.

Government revenue and expenditure

Seasonally adjusted quarterly general government revenue and expenditure, past 5 years, EUR billions. Source: Central Statistics Office (Table GFQ01).

Government balance, trailing four quarters

Overall balance (B9) and primary balance (overall balance excluding interest payments, i.e. B9 plus interest added back), trailing four quarters, not seasonally adjusted, past 5 years, EUR billions. Source: Central Statistics Office (Table GFQ01).

Debt ratios

General government gross debt as a share of GDP (official CSO annual ratio) and of Modified GNI (GNI*), past 5 years. GDP and GNI* are flattered by multinational activity; debt to GNI* is the more meaningful ratio. Debt to GDP: CSO Table GFA12. Debt to GNI*: derived by nevinomics from CSO Tables GFA13 (EDP face value debt) and NA001 (GNI*).

General government debt

Gross general government debt (EDP face value), end of quarter, EUR billions, past 5 years. Source: Central Statistics Office (Table GFQ02).

Interest expenditure

General government interest expenditure (excluding FISIM), trailing four quarters, not seasonally adjusted, EUR millions, past 5 years. Source: Central Statistics Office (Table GFQ01).

Interest expenditure, quarterly

General government interest expenditure (excluding FISIM), quarterly, not seasonally adjusted, EUR millions, past 5 years. Source: Central Statistics Office (Table GFQ01).
